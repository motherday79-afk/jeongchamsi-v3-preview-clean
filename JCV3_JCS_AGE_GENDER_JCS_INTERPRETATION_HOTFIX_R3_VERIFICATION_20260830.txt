JCV3 JCS AGE & GENDER — JCS INTERPRETATION HOTFIX R3
Date: 2026-08-30

ROOT CAUSE
- Previous engine used movementCap (5/10/15) as an absolute first-snapshot output cap.
- Therefore even materially different official cohort affinities were compressed into adjacent integers such as 5/6 or 6/7.
- cohortProfileEffect was also capped at only +/-4, so shared politician momentum dominated demographic structure.
- Low-confidence numeric estimates were converted to null, hiding data instead of exposing estimate + confidence.

R3 FIX
1. First-snapshot level is no longer clamped to the movement cap.
   - movement cap is used as a temporal change cap only when a healthy previous snapshot exists.
2. Official/proxy cohortAffinity is standardized relative to its own 12-cell distribution and mapped into a JCS structural cohort signal.
   - This preserves real source-derived demographic shape instead of copying one common NOW value.
3. Previous snapshots compressed to <=2 point total range are treated as invalid legacy shape and are structurally rebased.
4. For usable official/proxy baselines, low confidence no longer hides the numeric estimate.
   - Value is preserved; confidence remains the uncertainty indicator.
5. Calculation revision bumped to COHORT_JCS_INTERPRETATION_20260830_R3 so same-draft R2 snapshots are not silently reused.
